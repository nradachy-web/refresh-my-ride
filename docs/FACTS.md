## Three ways to get it done

- **Shop drop-off.** Enclosed bays, controlled lighting, indoor storage.
  REQUIRED for ceramic coating, window tint and paint correction.
- **Mobile.** Detailing at the customer's home or workplace. "We bring our own
  power and water." Needs a driveway or parking spot with room to walk the full
  perimeter of the vehicle. Good for interior details and Wash & Seal.
- **Valet.** They pick the vehicle up, service it at the shop, bring it back.
  "The option most of our coating customers choose when they can't spare the day."

Uber is covered for drop-off and pickup on ceramic coating jobs only.

## Ceramic coating (shop only)

- Packages: **The Refresh** (1 year), **The Revival** (3 years, most popular),
  **The Renewal** (5 years).
- Every package includes: decontamination (iron and tar removal, clay), paint
  correction before the coating, panel wipe, application by certified
  technicians, cure in the shop, a written warranty, an aftercare guide, and a
  free follow-up wash with inspection.
- Glass, wheels and trim can be coated in the same visit.
- **No ceramic price on the site.** It is quoted per vehicle (size, paint
  condition, how much correction). Their own pages disagree on a starting
  price, so none is published.
- Time: "at least a full day, longer when the paint needs more correction.
  We give you an exact window when we quote your vehicle." Say no more than that.
- It is not armor: it does not stop rock chips or key scratches.
- Brush-style automatic washes put swirls in any finish. Touchless is fine.

## Window tint (shop only)

- Film: Geoshield C2 Carbon and Geoshield C2 Ceramic. Both non-metalized (no
  interference with GPS, radio or phone signal). Lifetime warranty covering
  bubbling, peeling and fading. Blocks 99% of UV. Ceramic rejects far more
  infrared heat.
- Computer cut patterns on a plotter for the exact year, make and model. No
  blade touches the glass. Installed indoors.
- Prices ("from", standard coupe or sedan; SUVs and trucks quoted by vehicle):

  | Windows | Install time | C2 Carbon | C2 Ceramic |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Front 2 windows | about 1.5 hours | from $99.99 | from $149.99 |
  | Full sides | about 2.5 hours | from $199.99 | from $299.99 |
  | Full sides + rear glass | about 3.5 hours | from $299.99 | from $399.99 |
  | Front windshield | about 1.5 hours | ceramic film only | from $175.00 |

- Old tint removal: from $50 per area, 50% off when a full-vehicle tint is
  installed the same day. Defroster lines protected.
- Shades shown: 5%, 20% (most popular), 35%, 50%, 75%.
- Aftercare: leave windows up 3 to 4 days. Small water pockets or light haze in
  the first week are normal.
- Michigan tint law: say only that Michigan's rules are specific to each set of
  windows and differ from most states, that they walk the customer through what
  is legal for the vehicle (including darker rear windows and the medical

  which percentages are legal. Always "general information, not legal advice".
- Most installs are done the same day.

## Paint correction (shop only)

Their site has no dedicated page, so stay inside these lines:

- Machine polishing that removes swirl marks, light scratches, oxidation and
  water spots from the clear coat.
- Included in every ceramic coating package (done before the coating).
- Available on its own. The published entry points are **Polish & Protect**
  (exterior, from $344.99, one-step polish removing roughly 60 to 70% of light
  swirls, sealed 6 months) and **Luxury Refresh** (full detail with a one-step
  polish, from $439.99).
- Heavier work is a multi-stage ("2 step") correction, quoted per vehicle after
  they see the paint. Reviews mention 2 step corrections.
- Matt handles the heaviest correction work. Paint is inspected under shop
  lighting.
- Real examples on file: a 2021 Chevy Silverado 2500HD (heavy oxidation and
  water spots), a 1990 Mazda Miata (2 step correction), a 2019 Corvette Z06.
- Correction is not repainting: it cannot fix rock chips, deep scratches
  through the clear coat, or failed clear coat. Say so plainly.

- "IDA certified" or any named certification. Their words are "certified
  technicians" and "ceramic coating certified". Use those and nothing more.
- Customer counts for a town, customer stories, or anything about where staff live.
- Saturday or evening hours. Walk-ins. A phone number other than (586) 400-1303.

# Clinton Township, Macomb County, MI: research for the home town hub

Checked 2026-10-13. Every bullet is followed by the page it was confirmed on.
"Master Plan 2019" is the Charter Township of Clinton Master Plan 2019, the PDF
on the township's own site. Page numbers are the plan's printed page numbers.
Numbers below are for our reference. The content guide bans statistics and
rankings on pages, so use the plain fact and leave the figure out.

## Roads and borders

- The township covers 28.1 square miles. It is not the standard 6 mile by 6 mile township square because the City of Mount Clemens and the City of Fraser were both formed out of it. (Master Plan 2019, p. 11)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- It shares boundaries with Sterling Heights, Fraser, Roseville, Mount Clemens, St. Clair Shores, Harrison Township and Macomb Township. (Master Plan 2019, p. 11) Wikipedia's list of adjacent places adds Shelby Township and Chesterfield Township.
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Township,_Macomb_County,_Michigan
- South edge: Roseville has "14 Mile Road on the north border" and is bordered "to the north by Fraser and Clinton Township".
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roseville,_Michigan
- Gratiot Avenue (M-3) "crosses into Clinton Charter Township" north of 14 Mile Road. In the Detroit suburbs it is a boulevard with Michigan lefts at the major intersections. North of Metropolitan Parkway it splits into a one way pair toward Mount Clemens. The township's plan calls Gratiot south of Mount Clemens "an older commercial corridor" with "numerous curb cuts" and "site-specific parking lots". (Master Plan 2019, p. 43)
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-3_(Michigan_highway)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- Fraser is "bounded by 15 Mile Road, Kelly Road, 13 Mile Road, and Hayes Road", with Clinton Township to its north and northeast. So Kelly Road is the Fraser line at 14 Mile Road.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraser,_Michigan
- West edge: Hayes Road, shared with Sterling Heights. A county release describes "Hayes Road in Clinton Township and Sterling Heights" as Clinton Township's western border (project limits 18 Mile Road to Hall Road).
  https://macombgov.org/news/hayes-road-rehabilitation-work-start-macomb-county
- North edge: Hall Road (M-59). The Clinton Township part of the Hall Road corridor is "generally between Hayes Road and Interstate 94". (Master Plan 2019, p. 44) Groesbeck Highway (M-97) ends at Hall Road on the Macomb Township and Clinton Township line.
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-59_(Michigan_highway)
- The highest volume roads in the township are I-94, Hall Road (M-59), Gratiot Avenue (M-3), Groesbeck Highway (M-97), Metropolitan Parkway, Hayes Road and Garfield Road. The plan says the heaviest traveled roadways "offer a complete north-south or east-west connection through the Township". Its top ten list adds Cass Avenue, 19 Mile Road and 15 Mile Road. (Master Plan 2019, p. 27)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- "Clinton Township is often identified by its 3 main corridors: Groesbeck Highway, Gratiot Avenue, and Hall Road." It "is not characterized by a traditional downtown". (Master Plan 2019, pp. 42 to 43)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- Groesbeck Highway was built in the early 1900s to connect Mount Clemens to Detroit. "Historically, Groesbeck has been a center for automobile-related manufacturing and tooling establishments." (Master Plan 2019, p. 43) It passes through the southeast corner of Fraser, enters Clinton Township, crosses Metropolitan Parkway and the Clinton River, and ends at Hall Road.
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-97_(Michigan_highway)
- Garfield Road runs "From Utica Road in Fraser to 23 Mile Road in Macomb Township". An office corridor sits around Garfield Road and 19 Mile Road, near Henry Ford Macomb Hospital. (Master Plan 2019, p. 31)
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile_Road_System_(Detroit)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- I-94 passes through the township: the exit list places exit 234, Harper Avenue, in Clinton Township. Industrial land sits near I-94 between 14 Mile and 15 Mile Roads and along the Groesbeck corridor south of Mount Clemens. (Master Plan 2019, pp. 31 and 42)
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interstate_94_in_Michigan
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF

## Road agency and winter

- "Most roads in Clinton Township fall under the authority of the Macomb County Department of Roads (MCDR)."
  https://www.clintontownship.com/551/Sidewalks-Street-Trees-Rights-of-Way
- "For residential or subdivision roads within a township, the road is under Macomb County jurisdiction." State trunklines (roads with I, M or US, such as I-94, M-59 and M-3) belong to MDOT.
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/community-engagement/resources/frequently-asked-questions
- Snow removal is by priority: "All state and primary roads must be clear and safe for the motoring public before" the department starts on local and subdivision roads. It plows subdivision roads "when four inches or more of snow has fallen".
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/community-engagement/resources/frequently-asked-questions
- "Due to environmental regulations, MCDR only applies salt within subdivisions at entrances, exits, intersections and hills."
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/community-engagement/resources/frequently-asked-questions
- The county uses salt every winter: "more than 50,000 tons of salt each winter" on average, with plows starting on "main trunk line and primary road routes". (Release dated November 17, 2022. Earlier releases gave 57,000 and 49,500 tons.)
  https://www.macombgov.org/news/salt-equipment-and-crews-ready-macomb-county-prepared-winter
- The Department of Roads has four service centers, one of them in Clinton Township. Their duties include "Snow and ice removal".
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/departments-list/maintenance

## Housing pattern

- "Single-family homes occupy the largest share of the Township's land": about 48 percent, over 22,000 home sites, "primarily found in platted subdivisions and site condominium developments". (Master Plan 2019, p. 30)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- Multiple family housing (apartments, stacked condominiums, co-ops, assisted living) is "concentrated along the Hayes Road corridor, the Garfield Corridor, 19 Mile Road, and the eastern border of the Township, south of Metropolitan Parkway". (Master Plan 2019, p. 31)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- The township has four manufactured housing communities, three of them along North Gratiot. (Master Plan 2019, p. 30)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- The "vast majority" of the township's housing construction took place in the 1970s through the 1990s (Master Plan 2019, p. 16), and "Clinton Township is almost completely built" (p. 37). The 2024 plan repeats "almost completely built", and the planning director called it "a built community".
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
  https://www.candgnews.com/news/clinton-townships-master-plan-works-with-whats-available-6487

## Parks, river and trees

- "The Clinton River, for which the community is named, is formed from three branches within the township." It runs east into Harrison Township and on to Lake St. Clair.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_Township,_Macomb_County,_Michigan
- The largest wetland in the township lies along the North Branch of the Clinton River on both sides of Cass Avenue and "is substantially wooded". Wetlands along the Middle Branch are "primarily wooded", and the plan says that in many cases subdivisions or site condominiums "abut the wetland and river corridor area". (Master Plan 2019, p. 24)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF
- Township parks with rentable pavilions: Budd Park, Civic Center Park, Joy Park, Prince Drewry Park, Neil Reid Park, Normandy Park and George George Memorial Park. The parks page describes "playgrounds and picnic pavilions to nature trails and open fields". The 2019 plan counts 13 parks.
  https://www.clintontownship.com/302/Parks-Pavilions
- George George Memorial Park is on Moravian Drive and "borders the Clinton River". The township opened "a universal kayak and canoe launch" there on June 12, 2024, with a parking lot that has trailer spots and a riverside picnic area.
  https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2024/06/12/new-kayak-canoe-launch-opens-at-clinton-township-park/
- The township runs "our very own 1.5 acre fenced dog park". Use is by annual membership with a key fob.
  https://www.clintontownship.com/226/Dog-Park
- The Tomlinson Arboretum is "approximately 24 acres nestled between subdivisions in Clinton Township", with a bicycle and foot path past a wood lot. Its east entrance is on Romeo Plank Road across from the Clinton-Macomb Public Library.
  https://arboretum.ctwphc.org/
- The Civic Center is at 40700 Romeo Plank Road, south of Canal Road. The property includes a park, the senior center, a sledding hill and a historic village. (Master Plan 2019, p. 21)
  https://www.clintontownship.com/
  https://www.clintontownship.com/DocumentCenter/View/368/Clinton-Township-Master-Plan-PDF

## Landmarks and employers

- Macomb Community College's Center Campus is "located at the intersection of Garfield and Hall (M-59) roads", address 44575 Garfield Road. The college describes it as "densely wooded".
  https://macomb.edu/about-macomb/maps-directions/center-campus.html
- The Mall at Partridge Creek is "an open-air shopping center in Clinton Township" that opened October 18, 2007. It "welcomes dogs and provides four dog comfort stations on site".
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mall_at_Partridge_Creek
- Henry Ford Macomb Hospital is a 361 bed hospital at 15855 19 Mile Road.
  https://www.henryford.com/Locations/Macomb

## Drive

From docs/research/drive-times.json:

- measured_from: 40700 Romeo Plank Rd, Clinton Township, MI (the Civic Center)
- miles: 6.3
- minutes_no_traffic: 14

On the page: "about 6 miles" and "roughly 15 minutes without traffic", measured
from the Civic Center on Romeo Plank Road. The township is not in the content
guide's rounding table because it is the home town, so the same rounding used
for Sterling Heights (13 minutes written as roughly 15) is applied.

Route: confirmed. The public OSRM router (OpenStreetMap data) returns 6.29
miles and 13.75 minutes for that trip, which matches drive-times.json, by this
route: Romeo Plank Road south, Clinton River Road, Garfield Road south, east on
14 Mile Road, then left (north) onto Louise Avenue.
https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/driving/-82.9302914,42.5960358;-82.9260345,42.5403101?steps=true&overview=false

Two more approaches checked the same way:

- From Gratiot Avenue: west on 14 Mile Road, then right (north) onto Louise Avenue.
  https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/driving/-82.9029530,42.5413559;-82.9260345,42.5403101?steps=true&overview=false
- From Groesbeck Highway southbound: Kelly Road south to 14 Mile Road, east on 14 Mile Road, then left (north) onto Louise Avenue.

## Do not claim

- Winter brine or liquid de-icer on Clinton Township roads. The county's own pages confirm salt. They mention brine only for treating gravel roads in spring. Write "salt" when describing what the county does.
- Gravel or unpaved roads in Clinton Township. The county maintains about 335 miles of gravel roads countywide, but no source places any of them in this township.
- Who plows and salts the state trunklines (M-3, M-59, M-97, I-94). The county says state and primary roads come first, but not whose trucks do the state roads.
- Parking decks, open lots or valet parking at Partridge Creek, the college or the hospital. No source describes the type of parking, so no "parked in an open lot all day" claims.
- Street parking rules or a snow emergency parking ban. Nothing found for this township.
- Tree canopy figures, street tree species, or sap on any particular street. Only the wooded river corridors and the arboretum are sourced.
- Where the township dog park is. The township page gives its size and membership rules but not its location.
- Largest employers or employee counts. None found from a primary source.
- Gratiot Cruise attendance (sources conflict) or the 2026 date (not confirmed).
- "Most populous township in Michigan" (Wikipedia, 2020 census). It is a ranking, and the content guide bans rankings on pages.
- Any Huron-Clinton Metropark inside the township. Lake St. Clair Metropark is in Harrison Township.
- Rail dust or industrial fallout on vehicles near Groesbeck. The corridor's manufacturing history is sourced. Its effect on paint is not.
- The township's east boundary line and any I-94 exit other than Harper Avenue.

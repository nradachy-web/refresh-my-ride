# Shelby Township, MI: research fact sheet

Researched 2026-10-13 for the Refresh My Ride town pages. Every local statement
on the Shelby Township pages traces to a line below.

How to read the sources:

- "Fetched" means the page itself was opened and read.
- "Search excerpt" means the wording was read in a search result excerpt of
  that page. shelbytwp.org and parks.shelbytwp.org returned HTTP 403 to every
  direct fetch, so the township's own pages could only be read that way. Treat
  those lines as one step weaker than the fetched ones.

## Roads and borders

- Shelby Charter Township is in western Macomb County. Rochester and Rochester Hills (Oakland County) border it to the west. "The cities of Utica and Sterling Heights border the township to the south." The same article's adjacency box also lists Washington Township and Macomb Township as neighbors. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Charter_Township,_Michigan
- Utica is a separate city. It is "bordered to the north by Shelby Charter Township" and to the south by Sterling Heights. M-59 runs along Utica's southern border and M-53 crosses its east side. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utica,_Michigan
- Shelby Township's ZIP codes are 48315, 48316 and 48317. Utica's are 48315, 48317 and 48318. So the township and the city of Utica share 48315 and 48317. Fetched (both infoboxes). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Charter_Township,_Michigan and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utica,_Michigan
- M-53 is a north and south state highway maintained by MDOT. Near 18 Mile Road it separates from Van Dyke Avenue and becomes a four lane freeway, named the Van Dyke Freeway or Christopher Columbus Freeway, passing the east side of Utica in Shelby Township about one mile east of Van Dyke Avenue. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-53_(Michigan_highway)
- M-53 freeway exits in this stretch: exit 17 for M-59, exit 20 for 23 Mile Road, exit 23 for 26 Mile Road. The exit list places 26 Mile Road at the line between Shelby Township and Washington Township. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-53_(Michigan_highway)
- The same article describes the land along the freeway here: "The landscape is mostly residential subdivisions with parks and golf courses as the freeway exits Metro Detroit proper." Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-53_(Michigan_highway)
- South of the freeway, Van Dyke Avenue (M-53) crosses into Sterling Heights at 14 Mile Road and "passes next to the General Motors Technical Center" between 12 Mile and 14 Mile roads in Warren. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-53_(Michigan_highway)
- M-59 is an east and west state highway maintained by MDOT. "The M-59 freeway crosses the border between Sterling Heights and Shelby Township and ends at Van Dyke Avenue in Utica", where it becomes a boulevard called Hall Road. Hall Road is 20 Mile Road in the mile road system. To the west the freeway has an interchange with I-75 at Auburn Hills. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-59_(Michigan_highway)
- The Hall Road stretch of M-59 is described as a kind of main street for Macomb County, known for shopping and dining. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-59_(Michigan_highway)
- Shelby Township Hall and most township offices are at 52700 Van Dyke Ave, Shelby Township, MI 48316. This is the point docs/research/drive-times.json measures from. Search excerpt. https://www.shelbytwp.org/our-township/civic-center-campus

## Who looks after the roads, and winter

- The township's road page says all surface roads within Shelby Township are under the jurisdiction of the Macomb County Department of Roads, the Road Commission for Oakland County and the Michigan Department of Transportation, that the township has no jurisdiction to repair, expand or maintain roads, and that the Macomb County Department of Roads is the local authority for road projects, maintenance and snow removal. Search excerpt. https://shelbytwp.org/government/road-construction-maintenance
- The Macomb County Department of Roads (MCDR) Maintenance Department "has four service centers that serve all of Macomb County located in Washington Township, New Haven, Clinton Township and Shelby Township." Service center duties listed include "Snow and ice removal" and "Grading, brining and maintenance of gravel roads." Fetched. https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/departments-list/maintenance
- MCDR removes snow on a priority basis: "All state and primary roads must be clear and safe for the motoring public before the Department of Roads can begin snow removal on local and subdivision roads." The page also refers to "plowing and salting schedules". Fetched. https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/departments-list/maintenance
- "On average, MCDR uses more than 50,000 tons of salt each winter." The department reports a winter fleet of more than 100 snowplows, salt trucks and graders. This is a countywide figure. Fetched (releases dated December 1, 2021 and November 17, 2022). https://www.macombgov.org/news/macomb-county-department-roads-ready-ice-and-snow and https://www.macombgov.org/news/salt-equipment-and-crews-ready-macomb-county-prepared-winter
- MCDR "maintains approximately 335 miles of gravel roads throughout Macomb County", graded and treated as needed, with the gravel road program typically starting in May. This is countywide. No list of gravel roads inside Shelby Township was found (see "Do not claim"). Fetched. https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/departments-list/maintenance

## Housing pattern

- Census Bureau American Community Survey, 2024 1-year estimates, table B25024 (units in structure), Shelby charter township: 35,996 housing units. 20,269 are 1 unit detached (about 56%), 4,536 are 1 unit attached (about 13%), 9,066 are in buildings with 2 or more units (about 25%) and 2,125 are mobile homes (about 6%). Plain reading: detached houses are the most common home, with attached condos, apartment buildings and manufactured home communities making up the rest. Fetched (Census Reporter API, same data as the table page). https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/latest?table_ids=B25024&geo_ids=06000US2609972820 and https://censusreporter.org/profiles/06000US2609972820-shelby-charter-township-macomb-county-mi/

## Parks, water, trails and trees

- Stony Creek Metropark's address is 4300 Main Park Road, Shelby Township, MI 48316. It "offers 4,435 scenic acres" around "the gorgeous 500-acre Stony Creek Lake." Fetched. https://www.metroparks.com/stony-creek-metropark/
- The park "is predominantly in Washington Township and Oakland Township, with a small portion in Shelby Township." Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Creek_Metropark
- Stony Creek has two swimming beaches, Baypoint Beach and Eastwood Beach, plus a boat launch and boat rental. "No pets are allowed at either beach." Fetched. https://www.metroparks.com/stony-creek-metropark/
- Stony Creek mountain biking is "more than 14 miles of heavily wooded dirt single track and two track trails." Its nature trails "are comprised of areas of compact gravel, bare soil and mowed grasses." A paved hike and bike trail of about 6 miles loops the lake. Fetched. https://www.metroparks.com/stony-creek-metropark/
- At Stony Creek, "Dogs are allowed on the trails, but must be on a 6-foot leash at all times." Fetched. https://www.metroparks.com/stony-creek-metropark/
- River Bends Park is at 5700 22 Mile Road, Shelby Township, MI 48317. Macomb County lists Michigan Mountain Biking Association two track and single track trails there that "can be used year round", a surface of "Asphalt, natural", and amenities of disc golf, a barrier-free playground, pavilions, a nature center and a fishing site. Fetched. https://macombgov.org/make-macomb-your-home/outdoor-activities/trail-network/mountain-biking-and-equestrian-trails/river
- The township's own page says River Bends Park is divided into two sections by the Clinton River, which flows through the park for about six miles, and that the park has picnic shelters, river access points and miles of hiking trails. The western section is entered from Ryan Road and holds the nature center, a trap and archery range and a soccer field. Search excerpt. https://shelbytwp.org/government/departments/parks-and-recreation/river-bends-park-247
- "The township is drained by the Clinton River, a tributary of Lake St. Clair." Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Charter_Township,_Michigan
- The Macomb Orchard Trail "stretches from Richmond to Dequindre Road, where it connects to the Clinton River Trail." Length is given as 23.5 or 23.7 miles and the surface as asphalt and gravel. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macomb_Orchard_Trail
- The township says the Macomb Orchard Trail access point in Shelby Township is a parking lot on the west side of Dequindre Road, just south of 24 Mile Road. Search excerpt. https://www.shelbytwp.org/government/departments/parks-and-recreation/hike-and-bike-trails/macomb-orchard-trail
- The township describes nearly 1,200 acres of township parkland and more than 18 miles of nature trails, and names heron rookeries at Holland Ponds Park. Search excerpt. https://shelbytwp.org/our-township/visitor-information/suburban-wilderness
- Shelby Township is a Tree City USA community (its page says for 8 years) and has a tree and woodland preservation ordinance. The ordinance regulates trees inside woodland areas and, outside woodlands, trees seven inches or more in diameter, with a permit needed to remove or transplant trees in a woodland. Search excerpt. https://www.shelbytwp.org/our-township/keep-shelby-green/tree-city-usa and https://www.shelbytwp.org/our-township/keep-shelby-green/tree-ordinances

## Employers, schools and landmarks

- Amazon announced a one million square foot fulfillment center in Shelby Township on September 14, 2017, to open in 2018 and create 1,000 jobs. Local reporting placed the project on "the old Visteon site at 23 Mile and Mound Rd." Fetched. https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/amazon-to-create-1k-jobs-with-new-shelby-township-fulfillment-center and https://www.wxyz.com/news/region/macomb-county/amazon-eyes-old-visteon-facility-in-shelby-township-for-distribution-center
- Eisenhower High School is at 6500 25 Mile Road, Shelby Township, and belongs to Utica Community Schools. Most township residents are zoned to it, and a small number attend Romeo Community Schools. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisenhower_High_School_(Michigan) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shelby_Charter_Township,_Michigan
- Jimmy John's Field is at 7171 Auburn Road in downtown Utica, next door to the township. It opened May 30, 2016 and hosts the United Shore Professional Baseball League. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_John%27s_Field

## Car culture

- The Packard Proving Grounds is at 49965 Van Dyke Ave, Shelby Township, MI 48317. Packard Motor Car Company established it in 1927 with buildings by architect Albert Kahn and a 2.5 mile concrete oval test track with a timing tower. It is on the National Register of Historic Places. Fetched. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packard_Proving_Grounds
- The site is owned by the Packard Motor Car Foundation, a nonprofit, and its own site lists public events including an Annual Fall Open House (Sunday, October 25, 2026) and Packards & Pours. Fetched. https://www.packardprovinggrounds.org/
- Cars 'R' Stars is a "Car Show and Swap Meet" held at the Packard Proving Grounds. The page fetched describes the 14th year at the site, Sunday, September 14, 2025, and says "ALL MAKES, MODELS & YEARS ARE WELCOME ON GENERAL SHOW FIELD". An earlier report places the show "on Van Dyke South of 23 Mile Road in Shelby Township". No 2026 date was found. Fetched. https://www.packardprovinggrounds.org/cars-r-stars and https://www.oldcarsweekly.com/news/cars-r-stars-ppg-to-feature-fins-and-family-haulers

## Derived notes (geometry, not a new source)

- M-53 runs north and south (see above). A southbound morning drive therefore has the rising sun to the east, which is the driver's left. A northbound evening drive has the setting sun to the west, which is again the driver's left.
- M-59 and Hall Road run east and west (see above). Eastbound in the morning and westbound in the evening both face a low sun.

On the pages this is written as "about 13 miles from central Shelby Township"
and "roughly 25 minutes without traffic".

Route: confirmed. A query to the public OpenStreetMap routing engine (OSRM) on
2026-10-13, from the coordinates above to the shop (33115 Louise Ave geocodes
to lat 42.5403101, lon -82.9260345), returned 13.1 miles and 25 minutes, the
same numbers as drive-times.json, by this route:

1. Van Dyke Avenue south, about 4.8 miles.
2. Slight left onto Utica Road, southeast, about 6.6 miles.
3. Left onto East 14 Mile Road, heading east, about 1.4 miles.
4. Left onto Louise Avenue. The shop is on the north side of 14 Mile Road, just east of Kelly Road (from docs/FACTS.md).

The router's second choice was 24 Mile Road east to Garfield Road, Garfield
south about 10 miles to 14 Mile Road, then east: 15.4 miles and 27 minutes.

- Route query: https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/driving/-83.0308223,42.6821032;-82.9260345,42.5403101?overview=false&steps=true&alternatives=true
- Shop geocode: https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=33115+Louise+Ave,+Clinton+Township,+MI+48035&format=json&limit=3

Pages may describe the first route in plain words: Van Dyke Avenue south, Utica

## Do not claim

Looked for and could not confirm. None of this is on the pages.

- Gravel or limestone roads "above 25 Mile" or anywhere named in Shelby Township. The old site says so. MCDR's 2024 limestone program list names 21 road segments and none could be placed in Shelby Township, the county gives no gravel list by township, and OpenStreetMap tags only about ten short residential streets in the township as unpaved. No gravel road claim is made.
- Liquid brine or de-icer on Shelby Township roads in winter. County releases mention salt only. "Brining" appears on the county page only for gravel roads. A 2007 trade article about a brine maker at the old Road Commission of Macomb County is too old to lean on. Pages say salt for anything local.
- "Mature canopy in older subdivisions" or any claim about which neighborhoods are shaded. Only the Tree City USA status, the tree ordinance and the wooded parks are sourced.
- The township's rank for tree canopy in Macomb County. A search excerpt of the township's Arbor Day page says second largest, but it is a ranking and was not fetched. Not used.
- A dog park at River Bends Park. Official listings do not show one.
- River Bends Park acreage. The township excerpt says more than 850 acres and Wikipedia says 600. No acreage is stated.
- That Stony Creek's entrance is on 26 Mile Road. Only third party sites say so. The park's own page gives the Main Park Road address.
- That the Postal Service's preferred city name for 48315, 48316 and 48317 is Utica. Only ZIP aggregator sites say so. Pages rely on the shared ZIP codes shown in Wikipedia and nothing more.
- Boundary roads on the west and east (Dequindre Road, Hayes Road) and Hall Road as the full southern boundary. Not found stated in a fetched source.
- That Shelby Township "runs heavy on trucks, Suburbans and three row SUVs". No source. No vehicle mix is claimed.
- How many residents commute to the GM Technical Center or Auburn Hills. The roads lead there. Nobody counted who drives them.
- A ranked list of large employers, or KUKA. Only the Amazon fulfillment center was confirmed, from 2017 announcements, and it is not used on the pages.
- Whether the Lakeside Mall on Hall Road is open. Not checked, not mentioned.
- Parking decks or on-street parking as a pattern in Shelby Township. No source found.
- Open or unshaded parking lots at any named place. Not sourced, so pages say a car sits parked, not that it sits in an open lot.
- A 2026 date for Cars 'R' Stars. Pages name the show without a date.
- Customer counts, typical vehicles or "most booked" services for Shelby Township. The old site says these. They are not facts we can check.

# Sterling Heights, Michigan (Macomb County): research for the town pages

Checked 2026-10-13. Every bullet is a plain fact followed by the page it was confirmed on. Where a fact came only from a search result snippet and the page itself would not load, it is not listed here: it is under "Do not claim" at the bottom.

## Roads and borders

- Sterling Heights is bordered by Warren to the south, Madison Heights at its southwest corner, Troy to the west, Utica and Shelby Township to the north, Macomb Township at its northeast corner, Clinton Township to the east and Fraser to the southeast.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- It is described as a second-ring suburb, 14 to 20 miles north of downtown Detroit.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- East and west travel is mainly on the mile roads, from 14 Mile Road through 20 Mile Road (M-59). 16 Mile Road is also called Metro Parkway.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- M-59 is called Hall Road once the freeway ends, which happens at Van Dyke Avenue in Utica, where it becomes a boulevard. Farther east it is a six to eight lane divided highway. Wikipedia describes the Hall Road section as "somewhat of a main street in Macomb County", home to a wide variety of shopping and dining.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-59_(Michigan_highway)
- M-53 is Van Dyke Avenue. It crosses into Sterling Heights at 14 Mile Road. Near 18 Mile Road, M-53 separates from Van Dyke Avenue and becomes the Van Dyke Freeway (also named the Christopher Columbus Freeway). Just south of the city, between 12 Mile and 14 Mile roads in Warren, M-53 passes the General Motors Technical Center.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-53_(Michigan_highway)
- Mound Road is a north and south artery that runs continuously through the city.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- Utica Road is a diagonal connector that crosses the city from southeast to northwest.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- Dequindre Road is the border between Sterling Heights and Troy, and also the border between Macomb and Oakland counties.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- Hayes Road is the divider between Sterling Heights and Clinton Township.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- Fraser, the next city southeast, is bounded by 15 Mile Road, Kelly Road, 13 Mile Road and Hayes Road. So 14 Mile Road crosses Fraser from Hayes Road to Kelly Road, and the shop sits just past Kelly.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fraser,_Michigan

## Winter: who clears the roads, and salt

- The city's Department of Public Works clears major roadways first, then neighborhood roads. Its stated goal is to finish plowing all city streets, including courts and cul-de-sacs, within 30 to 36 hours after snowfall ceases.
  https://sterlingheights.gov/198/Snow-Ice-Removal-Program
- During a declared snow emergency all vehicles must be parked off city streets, and vehicles left on the street may be ticketed and towed. Snow emergencies are declared only when heavy snowfall impedes traffic on subdivision streets.
  https://sterlingheights.gov/198/Snow-Ice-Removal-Program
- The city's public works director is quoted saying a snow emergency does not end until every street in the city has been plowed curb to curb and salted.
  https://www.wxyz.com/news/what-it-means-to-be-under-a-snow-emergency
- One exception to the salting: roads completed during the 2022 to 2025 Local Road Program are plowed but not salted, to protect the new concrete from surface scaling or flaking.
  https://sterlingheights.gov/198/Snow-Ice-Removal-Program
  https://www.wxyz.com/news/no-salt-sterling-heights-using-new-method-to-preserve-newer-neighborhood-roads
- The county road agency is the Macomb County Department of Roads. It is responsible for more than 1,700 miles of county roads, uses more than 50,000 tons of salt in an average winter, and starts with main trunk line and primary road routes before working through local and residential roads. Its winter spending covers "primary, local and state roads".
  https://www.macombgov.org/news/salt-equipment-and-crews-ready-macomb-county-prepared-winter

## Housing and driveways

- The city's housing study says Sterling Heights "has a very large proportion of single-family homes". By unit count, single family detached and attached housing is 76.3% of all housing units and buildings with five or more units are 16.7%. 76% of occupied units are owner occupied. 52.1% of housing units were built between 1960 and 1979. (Numbers are for our reference. The content guide does not allow statistics on the pages.)
  https://www.sterlingheights.gov/2341/Housing-Study
- The city regulates residential driveways. Rules adopted in 2023 allow up to 45% of a front yard to be paved, allow horseshoe driveways on busier roads, and allow turnaround pads up to 200 square feet.
  https://www.candgnews.com/news/city-adopts-front-yard-paving-driveway-regulations-3438

## Parks, river, trees, water

- The Clinton River crosses the northeast part of the city, flowing east toward Lake St. Clair.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- The city says paddlers can kayak and canoe through nine miles of wildlife areas running through Sterling Heights. The two public launches are at Rotary Park (37328 Utica Road) and North Clinton River Park (8600 Riverland Drive).
  https://sterlingheights.gov/1434/Canoe-Kayak-the-Clinton-River
- Dodge Park is at 40620 Utica Road. It has paved and natural surface trails, a trail that runs parallel to the Clinton River, water access for kayaks and canoes, sports fields, a seasonal splash pad, a seasonal ice rink, pavilions, grills and playgrounds.
  https://www.macombgov.org/make-macomb-your-home/outdoor-activities/trail-network/mountain-biking-and-equestrian-trails/dodge
- The city's own facilities list confirms the Dodge Park Ice Rink and the Splash Pad in Dodge Park, plus a skate park, the Community Center and a Nature Center. The Nature Center is at 42700 Utica Rd, and the city describes a stroll along the river and an adjoining park there.
  https://sterlingheights.gov/1574/Facilities
  https://www.sterlingheights.gov/302/Nature-Center
- The city's off-leash Dog Park is at 41801 Ryan Rd. The all-dog area is 1.25 acres and the small-dog area is 0.75 acre. Entry is for annual pass holders. The facilities page places it at Delia Park.
  https://sterlingheights.gov/1722/Dog-Park
  https://sterlingheights.gov/1574/Facilities
- The Freedom Trail is a paved trail of nearly 10 miles that runs parallel to 16 Mile Road (Metro Parkway) from Schoenherr Road in Sterling Heights to Lake St. Clair Metropark in Harrison Township.
  https://macombgov.org/make-macomb-your-home/outdoor-activities/trail-network/non-motorized-paved-trails/freedom-trail
- The city has an estimated 37,500 trees on public streets and in city parks, and received its 37th consecutive Tree City USA award in April 2022.
  https://metromodemedia.com/sterling-heights-tree-canopy-22/
- The city has committed to planting 3,000 trees a year, including right-of-way plantings along roads, and is working against decades of tree loss from emerald ash borer and Dutch elm disease. (Source is labeled sponsored content, April 2026.)
  https://www.clickondetroit.com/live-in-the-d/2026/04/23/sterling-heights-goes-green/
- From Memorial Day through Labor Day the city has voluntary outdoor watering restrictions: odd and even days by address, and Public Works urges homeowners to water lawns between 11 p.m. and 5 a.m.
  https://sterlingheights.gov/207/Watering-Restrictions

## Employers, landmarks, schools

- Sterling Heights Assembly is at 38111 Van Dyke. It is operated by Stellantis North America and has built the Ram 1500 since 2018. The nearby Sterling Stamping plant opened in 1965.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights_Assembly
- A local TV report describes vehicles taken "from the parking lot" of the Sterling Heights Assembly Plant on Van Dyke Avenue. (Used only as evidence that vehicles are parked in a lot at the plant.)
  https://www.wxyz.com/news/two-vehicles-stolen-from-fca-plant-in-sterling-heights
- Ford's Van Dyke Electric Powertrain Center is in Sterling Heights. It began operation in 1968 and Ford lists its current products as transmissions and stampings.
  https://www.at.ford.com/us/en/home/news-media/plants/plant-details.van-dyke-transmission.html
- General Dynamics Land Systems is headquartered in Sterling Heights.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Dynamics_Land_Systems
- Many residents came to live in Sterling Heights to work in automobile plants operated by Chrysler and Ford.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan
- Lakeside Mall, on the M-59 commercial corridor, opened March 2, 1976 and closed permanently on July 1, 2024. The site is planned as a mixed-use development called Lakeside City Center.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakeside_Mall
- Public schools: Utica Community Schools serves the northern half of the city and Warren Consolidated Schools the southern half.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterling_Heights,_Michigan

## Car culture

- For 20 years the Fun Time Cruzers met Monday evenings, 4 to 8 p.m., May to September, at Lakeside Mall. The final cruise-in there was June 24, 2024, when about 500 classic cars filled the food court parking lot.
  https://candgnews.com/news/fun-time-cruzers-looking-for-a-new-location-6012
- As of May 28, 2025 the Fun Time Cruzers meet Mondays, 4 to 8 p.m., at NAPA Auto Parts on Van Dyke Avenue north of 21 Mile Road in Shelby Township. The same article lists weekly cruise nights on every day of the week across Macomb County (Warren, Center Line, Roseville, St. Clair Shores, Chesterfield Township and others). None of the listed events is in Sterling Heights.
  https://www.candgnews.com/news/retro-rides-rev-up-local-car-shows--8436

## Drive

From docs/research/drive-times.json:

- Measured from: 40555 Utica Rd, Sterling Heights, MI
- Miles: 6.6
- Minutes without traffic: 13
- Page wording, per the content guide table: "about 7 miles from central Sterling Heights" and "roughly 15 minutes without traffic".

Route: confirmed. I ran the same start point through the public OSRM router (OpenStreetMap data, router.project-osrm.org) on 2026-10-13 to the shop at 33115 Louise Ave. It returned 6.6 miles and 13 minutes, matching drive-times.json, by this route: Utica Road southeast for about 4.6 miles, left (east) on 14 Mile Road for about 1.4 miles, left (north) on Louise Avenue.

The same router from other points in the city:

- 14 Mile Road at Van Dyke (south edge): 14 Mile Road east the whole way.
- 14 Mile Road at Dequindre (southwest corner): 14 Mile Road east the whole way.
- Hall Road at Van Dyke (north edge): Van Dyke south, Utica Road southeast, 14 Mile Road east.
- Mound Road at Metro Parkway: Metro Parkway east, Utica Road southeast, 14 Mile Road east.

So two simple descriptions are safe on the pages: "14 Mile Road east" for the south end of the city, and "Utica Road southeast to 14 Mile Road, then east" for the middle and north. The shop is on the north side of 14 Mile Road, just east of Kelly Road. The extra mileages the router gave for those other start points are not in drive-times.json and must not be printed on the pages.

Note for the orchestrator: the existing hub and ceramic coating pages call the trip "a straight run east on 14 Mile Road". That is exact for the south edge of the city. From the measured central point the router spends most of the trip on Utica Road and only the last stretch on 14 Mile.

## Do not claim

- Brine on any named road. The county press releases mention salt only. A 2021 Macomb Daily article that reportedly says the county uses "a saltwater brine in its mix" appeared only as a search snippet, and the page itself returned an error. Keep to "road salt" for local statements.
- Which agency salts M-53 and M-59. They are state routes. The county says only that its winter spending covers "primary, local and state roads". Not confirmed beyond that.
- Gravel or unpaved public roads. Searched, found nothing. Do not mention gravel or dust from unpaved roads.
- Parking decks or parking structures. Found no source for any in the city.
- Street parking habits, or any overnight street parking rule other than the snow emergency rule. Only a third-party site, not the city code.
- That plant employee parking is uncovered, or how many shifts or employees the plants have. One news report says "parking lot" and nothing more. Employee counts differ between sources. Keep any statement about parking at work conditional.
- Where Sterling Heights residents commute to. No source.
- A current car show or cruise inside Sterling Heights. The Monday cruise-in left when Lakeside Mall closed. A "Pomeroy Classic Car Show" appears only on a third-party event listing. The Woodward Dream Cruise is in Oakland County.
- Lakeside Mall as a place people shop or park today. It closed July 1, 2024.
- "Second largest city in Macomb County." Wikipedia calls it the second largest suburb of Detroit and the fourth most populous city in Michigan. No county rank was found, and rankings are not allowed on the pages anyway.
- The Clinton River Park Trail's length and surface (reported as 10.2 miles of asphalt). The TrailLink page would not load. Seen only in a search snippet.
- A Huron-Clinton Metropark in the city. There is none. The nearest mention is Lake St. Clair Metropark, at the far end of the Freedom Trail in Harrison Township.
- Lakes. Found no lake of note inside the city, so no beach sand or lake air.
- Freedom Hill County Park details. No official county page found, only venue listings and a 2003 legislative analysis.
- Hard water, or what is in the city's tap water. Not researched. The pages say only that sprinkler water left to dry on paint leaves spots.
- Weather numbers of any kind.

# West Bloomfield, Michigan: research fact sheet

Charter Township of West Bloomfield, Oakland County. Researched 2026-10-13 for the Refresh My Ride town pages. Each fact is followed by the page it was confirmed on. Where a fact was only seen in a search excerpt and the page itself could not be opened, it says so.

Care with names: Bloomfield Township is a different municipality with its own road department and its own tree ordinance. Nothing from bloomfieldtwpmi.gov is used here.

## Borders and main roads

- West Bloomfield is a charter township in south-central Oakland County. It borders Bloomfield Township to the east, Farmington Hills to the south and Commerce Township to the west. To the north are Waterford Township, Orchard Lake Village, Keego Harbor and Sylvan Lake.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- Orchard Lake Road runs north to south through the middle of the township. Wikipedia says it "is the main artery of West Bloomfield's economy along with West Maple Road (15 Mile Rd.)", and is "flanked on each side by long strip malls and shopping complexes". The strip mall wording is attached to Orchard Lake Road. The pages use it for Orchard Lake Road only and call West Maple the other main business road.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- The mile roads are the east-west roads of the metro Detroit grid. 15 Mile Road is Maple Road and runs from Harper to Milford Road. 14 Mile Road runs from Harper to Walled Lake "with brief interruption in Beverly Hills".
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roads_and_freeways_in_metropolitan_Detroit
- Northwestern Highway (M-10) runs into the southeast of the township for about an eighth of a mile and ends at Orchard Lake Road. It is the only designated highway that enters the township. It is NOT a freeway here: "Northwest of this interchange, M-10 transitions to a boulevard with Michigan lefts", the interchange being the one with I-696 near Telegraph Road, where the Lodge Freeway ends. "The northern terminus is in West Bloomfield Township at the intersection with Orchard Lake Road."
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-10_(Michigan_highway)
- M-5 (the Haggerty Connector) passes about four tenths of a mile west of the township's border with Commerce Township along 14 Mile Road, which gives the township a close connection to I-275, I-696 and I-96. M-5 has direct ramps to I-696 and to I-96 east and I-275 south. Near the township it is an expressway, not a full freeway: "In 1999, a second extension of M-5 was completed to 14 Mile Road, but only as an expressway." It becomes a full freeway just south of 13 Mile Road.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-5_(Michigan_highway)
- Because neither road is a freeway where it meets the township, the pages say "highway", not "freeway", and say only that both lead to I-696.
- Telegraph Road (US-24) runs about a third of a mile northeast of the township's northeast corner. It does not enter the township.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- Roads the township's own community information page uses in addresses and directions: Walnut Lake Road (Town Hall is at 4550 Walnut Lake Road), Maple Road, Orchard Lake Road, Middlebelt Road, Farmington Road, Drake Road, Haggerty Road, Commerce Road, Pontiac Trail and Cooley Lake Road.
  https://wbtownship.org/visitors/community_information/index.php
- Haggerty Road is on the west side: the West Bloomfield Trail "extends from Sylvan Manor Park in the east to Haggerty Road in the west".
  https://wbparks.org/parks-facilities/west-bloomfield-trail/

## Who looks after the roads, and winter salt

- "Public roads are under the jurisdiction of the Road Commission for Oakland County (RCOC)". "Private Roads are the responsibility of the property owners."
  https://wbtownship.org/visitors/community_information/index.php
- "All roads in West Bloomfield are managed by the Road Commission of Oakland County. This includes repairing them and keeping them clear. The township does not have its own Department of Public Works." The county garage that serves West Bloomfield is in Waterford Township. (West Bloomfield Beacon, C and G Newspapers, February 22, 2026.)
  https://www.candgnews.com/news/officials-take-stock-of-the-snowy-winter-season-10274
- The Road Commission clears by traffic volume and speed. Its spokesman named Orchard Lake Road as a safety priority over a subdivision street, and the article says "Middlebelt Road is another road given priority for snow removal in West Bloomfield." The article does not mention brine. The brine fact comes from the Road Commission brochure below.
  https://www.candgnews.com/news/officials-take-stock-of-the-snowy-winter-season-10274
- The Road Commission's winter brochure puts "critical priority" roads at the top of the list, then "priority 1" roads, and says: "After clearing higher-volume roads, crews move to neighborhood or subdivision roads".
  https://www.rcocweb.org/187/Winter-Maintenance
- The Road Commission salts paved roads in winter. Its winter maintenance brochure (updated 12/25) says guidelines call for 400 pounds of salt per two-lane mile, and that it "does often spray main roads with liquid brine, or salt water, ahead of time, which slows the formation of ice".
  https://www.rcocweb.org/187/Winter-Maintenance
- Subdivision streets are the last priority in the Road Commission's written policy ("Priority V: All other roads, including subdivision streets"). It "does not plow subdivision streets unless at least four inches of snow have fallen", and after a major storm subdivision streets in townships "may not be plowed for one or more days".
  https://www.rcocweb.org/DocumentCenter/View/8916/Plowing-Subdivisions-in-Oakland-County-Townships
- The Road Commission also maintains more than 230 miles of state highway in the county for MDOT, including I-696 and Telegraph Road.
  https://www.rcocweb.org/DocumentCenter/View/8916/Plowing-Subdivisions-in-Oakland-County-Townships

## Gravel roads

- The township has residents on gravel roads: its community information page says "Residents on gravel roads have until April 29 to apply for the DUST CONTROL PROGRAM offered by the Road Commission for Oakland County." No count or list of gravel roads in the township was found.
  https://wbtownship.org/visitors/community_information/index.php
- County-wide, the Road Commission has more than 750 miles of gravel road. "Salt cannot be used on gravel roads because it soaks in and will not bond to the gravel surface", so "Sand is spread on curves and corners for traction." In summer "crews spray calcium chloride brine four times throughout the summer's dust season on some gravel roads".
  https://www.rcocweb.org/185/Gravel-Roads
- The winter brochure agrees: "Sand is used on gravel roads where there are no storm sewers and salt is less effective."
  https://www.rcocweb.org/187/Winter-Maintenance
- Chloride for dust control on local and subdivision gravel roads is only applied "when paid for by the township, homeowners or a combination of both."
  https://www.rcocweb.org/339/Gravel-Road-Dust-Control-and-Chloride-Sc

## Lakes

- West Bloomfield "is sometimes referred to by its residents as the lake township of Oakland County", being "heavily dotted with small and medium-sized lakes". The Census Bureau figure is 31.2 square miles in total, of which 3.9 square miles is water.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- Cass Lake, the largest lake in the county, is partly in West Bloomfield. Pine Lake lies completely within the township. Orchard Lake, directly west of Pine Lake, is surrounded by the separate City of Orchard Lake Village.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- "Cass Lake is the largest and deepest lake in Oakland County." It is bordered by Waterford Township, West Bloomfield Township, Orchard Lake Village and Keego Harbor, which puts it on the north side of West Bloomfield.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_Lake_(Michigan)
- Walnut Lake is a private, all-sports lake in West Bloomfield Township with homes along its shoreline. Pine Lake is also private and all-sports, with homes along its shoreline.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walnut_Lake_(West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan)
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_Lake_(West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan)
- Walnut Lake is wholly inside the township. Wikipedia only says it is "in West Bloomfield Township", so this was checked on OpenStreetMap (queried 2026-10-13): the lake's bounding box runs from longitude -83.3416 to -83.3225, and the township's east boundary is at -83.3209.
  https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=Walnut+Lake,+West+Bloomfield+Township,+Michigan&format=json
  https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=West+Bloomfield+Township,+Oakland+County,+Michigan&format=json

## Parks, trails and tree cover

- "West Bloomfield Parks oversees 12 parks and facilities and nearly 600 acres of public parkland."
  https://www.wbparks.org/parks/
- Marshbank Park, 2805 Hiller Road, is "over 80 acres in size" and "sits along the shoreline of beautiful Cass Lake, the largest lake in Oakland County". It has a fishing pier, a canoe and kayak launch, a sledding hill, a soccer field, a paved walking path and a nature trail.
  https://wbparks.org/parks-facilities/marshbank-park/
- West Bloomfield Woods Nature Preserve, 4655 Arrowhead Road, is a 162 acre preserve with "mature oak and hickory trees", unpaved trails, wetlands with an overlook and a parking lot. It was "the first in Michigan to receive recognition as an Urban Wildlife Sanctuary". "No bikes are allowed in the Preserve", so a page must not put bikes on the preserve trails.
  https://wbparks.org/parks-facilities/west-bloomfield-woods-nature-preserve/
- The West Bloomfield Trail is a former railroad corridor that "meanders for 6.8 miles through West Bloomfield, Orchard Lake, Keego Harbor and Sylvan Lake". Its central trailhead, with a restroom, is at 4655 Arrowhead Road, the same address as the nature preserve, whose page lists a parking lot. Bikes are allowed on the trail: the page lists bike racks and bike repair stations.
  https://wbparks.org/parks-facilities/west-bloomfield-trail/
- Trail surface: TrailLink describes it as a "crushed-limestone trail". Seen in search excerpts only. TrailLink refused a direct fetch, and the parks page does not name the surface. Not used on the pages.
  https://www.traillink.com/trail/west-bloomfield-trail
- The West Bloomfield Dog Park is inside Karner Farm at 5911 Halsted Road. It "offers nearly 4 acres of fenced-in area" in three sections and is "Open daily, year-round from 8 am to sunset except on Wednesdays when it closes from 8 to 11 am for maintenance". Entry needs a registration and a key fob.
  https://wbparks.org/parks-facilities/west-bloomfield-dog-park/
- The township regulates trees in designated woodlands, delineated wetlands and a 25 foot Environmental Features Setback around wetlands and watercourses. "Any cutting of trees, shrubs, ground foliage, etc is not permitted inside of a designated woodland" without going to the Environmental Department first. "If the Township determines that a tree is not dead, dying or diseased, a permit will be required." The rules reach private lots: "If your property is located within a regulated area, you must contact the Planning & Development Services Department". Outside those areas the township does not regulate tree cutting. Neither page says how much of the township is designated woodland, so the pages speak only of a car that is parked under branches.
  https://wbtownship.org/government/building,_planning___development_services/environmental/frequently_asked_questions.php
  https://wbtownship.org/government/building,_planning___development_services/environmental/tree_trimming_removal.php

## Housing and land use

- Oakland County's 2025 Land Use Statistics sheet for the Township of West Bloomfield lists single family residential as the largest land use (8,512.0 acres, 19,074 parcels). Multiple family is 1,026.6 acres. Water is 3,413.5 acres and recreation or conservation land is 2,557.0 acres. Supports "mostly single family homes". It says nothing about driveways, garages or street parking.
  https://cdn.oaklandcountymi.gov/oakland-sitefinity-prod/docs/default-source/economic-development/economic-development/planning/current-land-use-statistics/sum2025-x.pdf?sfvrsn=cdc8cf6e_0

## Employers, shopping and landmarks

- Henry Ford West Bloomfield Hospital is at 6777 W. Maple. The township's 2026 budget lists it as the largest employer in the township, followed by West Bloomfield Schools and the township itself.
  https://wbtownship.org/visitors/community_information/index.php
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- Parking at the hospital is self-parking "in our parking structure, near the Main entrance, and at the Henry Ford Cancer entrance". The parking structure opened in 2023. The page does not say where staff park, so the pages do not say that hospital cars sit in the sun. They say only that the hospital is on West Maple.
  https://henryford.com/locations/west-bloomfield/about/campus/parking
- West Bloomfield High School and the Frankel Jewish Academy are in the township. Seven school districts serve the township.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bloomfield_Township,_Michigan
- The township lists two public golf courses: Bay Pointe at 4001 Haggerty Road and Shenandoah at 5600 Walnut Lake Road.
  https://wbtownship.org/visitors/community_information/index.php
- The township maintains 68 miles of pedestrian "Safety Paths" along its roads.
  https://wbtownship.org/government/building,_planning___development_services/engineering/safety_paths/index.php

## Drive

From docs/research/drive-times.json:

- measured_from: 4550 Walnut Lake Rd, West Bloomfield, MI (Town Hall)
- miles: 25.1
- minutes_no_traffic: 45

On the pages: "about 25 miles from central West Bloomfield" and "roughly 45 minutes without traffic".

Route not confirmed. There is no single main road from West Bloomfield to 14 Mile Road and Kelly Road. A public routing engine (router.project-osrm.org, queried 2026-10-13, Town Hall to 33115 Louise Ave) returned two different multi-road routes of almost the same length: one east on Walnut Lake Road, Quarton Road, Big Beaver Road and Metropolitan Parkway, then south on Utica Road to 14 Mile Road (25.2 miles, 45 minutes), and one via Orchard Lake Road, Northwestern Highway and I-696 (28.8 miles, 47 minutes). From the south side of the township it preferred the I-696 route. A route forced along 14 Mile Road itself was slower (52 minutes) and left 14 Mile Road for side streets in Beverly Hills, which matches Wikipedia's note that 14 Mile Road has a "brief interruption in Beverly Hills". Because the sensible route depends on where in the township the driver starts, the pages describe no route. They give the distance, the time and the shop's location only: north side of 14 Mile Road, just east of Kelly Road.

West Bloomfield is the far edge of the service area. FACTS.md gives mobile and valet reach as "about 30 to 40 minutes" from Clinton Township and lists West Bloomfield as an open question. The pages do not promise a mobile visit or a valet pickup. They say to tell us where you are and we will confirm.

## Do not claim

Looked for and could not confirm. None of this is on the pages.

- Winding or hilly roads. No road agency or township source describes the roads that way.
- A tree canopy figure, or that the township as a whole is "heavily wooded". Only the woodland rules and the 162 acre preserve are sourced.
- How many lakes are in the township. Sources say "heavily dotted" and give no count.
- A public beach or a public boat launch for trailered boats in the township. Marshbank Park has a canoe and kayak launch and a fishing pier. Dodge 4 State Park on Cass Lake is in Waterford Township.
- Which streets are gravel, or how many. Only that some residents live on gravel roads.
- That every home has a driveway or garage, or anything about condos, parking decks or street parking. The only parking deck confirmed is the hospital's.
- A current car show, cruise or cars and coffee in West Bloomfield. The Orchard Lake Road cruise is from 2005. The Woodward Dream Cruise is not in the township.
- Salt tonnage or snow totals for the township. County-wide figures exist and weather numbers are not allowed on the pages anyway.
- Income, home values or "affluent". Sources use that language. The pages do not.
- A route to the shop. See "Drive".
- That mobile detailing or valet pickup reaches West Bloomfield.
- That Northwestern Highway or M-5 is a freeway at the township. One is a boulevard and the other an expressway there. Say "highway".
- That West Maple Road is lined with strip malls. The source attaches that wording to Orchard Lake Road.
- That cars at the hospital are parked in the sun. It has a parking structure as well as self-parking near the entrances.
- That oak and hickory stand over residents' driveways. Oak and hickory are sourced for the nature preserve only.
- That salt "bonds" to clear coat. The shop's own wording is that salt, brine and slush "don't get to sit against bare clear coat" on a coated car.

## Fact check, 2026-10-13

An independent pass re-opened these sources and confirmed the wording quoted above: the Wikipedia township page (borders, Orchard Lake Road, Maple Road as 15 Mile, Northwestern Highway, M-5, Telegraph, Cass Lake, Pine Lake, the lake township nickname, the hospital as top employer), the C and G Newspapers article of February 22, 2026 (Road Commission runs every road, no township public works department, Orchard Lake Road and Middlebelt Road as priorities), the Road Commission winter brochure, updated 12/25 (brine sprayed ahead of time, 400 pounds per two-lane mile, sand on gravel roads), the Road Commission subdivision plowing brochure, updated 3/23 (Priority V, the four inch rule), the Road Commission gravel roads page (750 miles, no salt on gravel, sand on curves and corners, calcium chloride brine four times a summer), the township community information page (gravel road dust control notice, road jurisdiction, hospital address), both township tree pages, and the West Bloomfield Parks pages for Marshbank Park, the nature preserve, the trail, the dog park and the parks total.

Corrections made to the pages after that pass:

- "Freeway miles" became "highway miles", because Northwestern Highway is a boulevard and M-5 an expressway where they meet the township.
- Strip malls are now attributed to Orchard Lake Road only.
- "The preserve is mature oak and hickory" became "has mature oak and hickory trees", and sap is now tied to a car parked under branches, not to every car in the township.
- "Marshbank Park sits on more than 80 acres of Cass Lake shoreline" became "is more than 80 acres on the Cass Lake shoreline". The park is 80 acres. The shoreline is not.
- "A launch on Cass Lake" became "a canoe and kayak launch", so nobody reads it as a boat ramp.
- "Residents call" became "Residents sometimes call", to match the source.

Not re-opened in this pass: the Oakland County land use sheet, the Wikipedia roads and freeways page, the safety paths page and the 2005 cruise listing. None of them is the only support for a sentence on the pages.

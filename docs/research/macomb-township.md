# Macomb Township, Macomb County, MI: research for the town pages

Checked 2026-10-13. Every bullet is followed by the page it was confirmed on.
Numbers are here so a claim can be checked. The pages themselves do not print
statistics (see docs/CONTENT-GUIDE.md).

## Where it is and what borders it

- Macomb Township is at the geographic center of Macomb County. Mount Clemens, the county seat, is 7 miles south. Total area is 36.35 square miles, of which 0.09 square miles (0.26%) is water.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macomb_Township,_Michigan
- Hall Road (M-59) is the south line, shared with Clinton Township. The M-97 article places the north end of Groesbeck Highway at M-59/Hall Road "on the Clinton Township and Macomb Township line" (their wording uses a dash between the two names).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-97_(Michigan_highway)
- The county lists a project on "Card Road, from Hall Road (M-59) to 350 feet north" under Macomb Township, which also puts Hall Road on the township's south edge.
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/news/macomb-county-department-roads-projects-starting-summer
- Hayes Road is the west line, shared with Shelby Township. WDIV reported work "on the border of Shelby Township and Macomb Township" on Hayes Road from 21 Mile to 23 Mile Road.
  https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/2015/01/23/road-closures-in-macomb-county
- 26 Mile Road is the north line. The county lists the 26 Mile Road and Hayes Road intersection as a Macomb Township project shared with Ray, Shelby and Washington townships, and Wikipedia puts the community of Meade "on the boundary with Ray Township at 26 Mile Road and North Avenue".
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/macomb-work-zone
- Chesterfield Township is the neighbor to the east ("on the west by Macomb Township").
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chesterfield_Township,_Michigan

## The roads people drive

- The township's own history page lists its mile roads: 20 Mile Road is "M-59, Hall Road or Rosso Highway", followed by 21, 22, 23, 24, 25 and 26 Mile Roads.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/325/History-of-Macomb
- Romeo Plank Road is a historic route that follows the Middle Branch of the Clinton River on its western side. The old settlement of Macomb Corners is at Romeo Plank and 25 Mile Road. Waldenburg is in the 22 Mile Road area.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macomb_Township,_Michigan
- Romeo Plank Road runs from Clinton River Road to 25 Mile. Garfield Road runs "from Utica Road in Fraser to 23 Mile Road in Macomb Township", with construction continuing toward 25 Mile Road.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile_Road_System_(Detroit)
- The mile roads are the east to west roads of a one mile grid: "Many of the grid's east-west roads are known by numbers", on "a series of local roads spaced one mile apart on a perpendicular grid". So 21 Mile through 26 Mile and Hall Road all run east and west. The same article calls Hayes Road "a township border road through eastern Macomb County" and says the Hall Road segment of M-59 "comprises the busiest commercial corridor in Macomb County" (a ranking: do not print it, say shopping centers).
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile_Road_System_(Detroit)
- East of Van Dyke Avenue in Utica, M-59 "becomes a boulevard called Hall Road". Wikipedia describes the stretch as "somewhat of a main street in Macomb County" with "a wide variety of shopping and dining", naming Lakeside Mall in Sterling Heights and The Mall at Partridge Creek in Clinton Township.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-59_(Michigan_highway)
- Groesbeck Highway (M-97) ends at Hall Road and continues north into the township as North Avenue.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-97_(Michigan_highway)
- The township supervisor said of Romeo Plank Road that "on busy days it's backed up a mile heading south" (WXYZ, 2025). The same report says the township "has grown faster than any community in the state of Michigan over the last 30 years" and that roads have lagged behind.
  https://www.wxyz.com/news/voices/macomb-twp-set-to-make-historic-investment-in-road-improvement
- Romeo Plank Road between 21 1/2 Mile and 23 Mile Road is planned to be widened from two lanes to five. As of August 2026 the project was waiting on a federal permit and had not started. Time sensitive: do not print a start date.
  https://www.wxyz.com/news/voices/romeo-plank-road-widening-project-stalled-for-years-leaving-boarded-up-homes-and-closed-businesses-in-macomb-township
- County road work listed in the township for 2026: Broughton Road reconstruction between 23 1/2 Mile and 24 1/2 Mile Road (July to November 2026, with 24 Mile Road closed to through traffic between Romeo Plank and Foss), an asphalt overlay on 21 Mile Road from Garfield to Romeo Plank, a 21 Mile Road bridge replacement, and the 26 Mile and Hayes intersection. Time sensitive: the pages may say road work is ongoing, not name a closure.
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/macomb-work-zone

## Who looks after the roads, and winter

- Roads in a township, including residential and subdivision streets, are under Macomb County Department of Roads (MCDR) jurisdiction. Roads with an I, M or U.S. number, M-59 among them, are state trunklines that belong to MDOT. MCDR has no jurisdiction over private roads.
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/community-engagement/resources/frequently-asked-questions
- Snow removal is by priority: "All state and primary roads must be clear and safe for the motoring public before the Department of Roads can begin snow removal on local and subdivision roads."
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/departments-list/maintenance
- Inside subdivisions the county salts very little: "MCDR only applies salt within subdivisions at entrances, exits, intersections and hills." Subdivision roads are plowed "when four inches or more of snow has fallen". (Do not print the four inch figure: the guide bars weather numbers.)
  https://www.macombgov.org/departments/department-roads/community-engagement/resources/frequently-asked-questions
- MCDR says it uses more than 50,000 tons of salt in an average winter and runs a winter fleet of more than 100 snowplows, salt trucks and graders (November 2022 release; earlier releases give 49,500 and 57,000 tons).
  https://www.macombgov.org/news/salt-equipment-and-crews-ready-macomb-county-prepared-winter
- Brine: the county applies brine to gravel roads twice a year for maintenance. Winter brine on paved roads was confirmed only in a 2007 trade article about the Road Commission of Macomb County's brine maker for pre-wetting. Treat "salt" as confirmed for the county and "brine" as general Michigan wording only.
  https://www.roadsbridges.com/home/article/10583866/automatic-control

## Housing and growth

- Census ACS 2024 (1 year): 33,497 housing units, of which 26,597 are detached single unit houses and 3,674 are attached single unit homes. 30,253 of 32,969 occupied homes are owner occupied. Plain reading: most homes are detached houses, with a real minority of attached homes and condos.
  https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/latest?table_ids=B25024,B25003,B25034&geo_ids=06000US2609950480
- Same source, year built: 27,100 of the 33,497 homes were built in 1990 or later, and 1,141 were built in 2020 or later. Plain reading: most of the township's houses went up in the last 35 years and building has not stopped.
  https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/latest?table_ids=B25024,B25003,B25034&geo_ids=06000US2609950480
- Population was 50,478 in 2000, 79,580 in 2010 and 91,663 in 2020. It was the most populous civil township in Michigan as of 2020.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macomb_Township,_Michigan
- A 2022 township press release on its master plan ("Cultivate Macomb!") says that since 2020 "over one thousand new residential and commercial spaces have been approved" and "shovels are in the ground building more residential units every day".
  https://www.macomb-mi.gov/Archive.aspx?ADID=1808
- In February 2023 the township board approved the McBride Crossing planned unit development on agricultural land along 24 Mile Road near the planned Broughton Road extension, with townhouses along Broughton.
  https://candgnews.com/news/macomb-trustees-approve-future-broughton-pud-1813
- The township history page says the "flat land and fertile soil brought in by the Clinton River attracted many German farmers", and that the township is "now on the cutting edge of urban growth". Plain reading: this was farmland, and it is flat.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/325/History-of-Macomb

## Parks, water and what they put in a car

- Macomb Corners Park is a 94 acre (35 developed) sports complex on the north side of 25 Mile Road just east of Luchtman Road: 6 lighted ball diamonds, 8 soccer fields, 2 sand volleyball courts, a one mile walk and run trail. It hosts the Summer Concert Series and Movie Under the Stars.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/454/Macomb-Corners-Park
- Pitchford Park is 14.1 acres on Romeo Plank Road, a quarter mile north of 22 Mile Road. It opened in November 2023 and has a large dog park area and a small dog park area.
  https://www.macomb-mi.gov/771/Pitchford-Park
- Waldenburg Park is about 17 acres on 21 Mile Road, a quarter mile east of Romeo Plank Road, with a play area, picnic pavilions, a basketball court and a walking trail.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/455/Waldenburg-Park
- The township also lists Town Center Park (15 acres, south side of 25 Mile Road just west of Broughton Road), Lucido River Park (North Avenue, just south of 24 Mile) and the 26 Mile Road Nature Preserve.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/368/Our-Parks
- The Middle Branch of the Clinton River runs past Macomb Corners and Waldenburg. There is no lake of any size: water is 0.26% of the township's area.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macomb_Township,_Michigan

## Schools, landmarks and events

- Four public school districts cover the township: Chippewa Valley, L'Anse Creuse, New Haven and Utica.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macomb_Township,_Michigan
- Dakota High School (Chippewa Valley Schools) is at 21051 21 Mile Road in Macomb Township.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakota_High_School_(Michigan)
- L'Anse Creuse High School North is at 23700 21 Mile Road in Macomb Township.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L%27Anse_Creuse_High_School_-_North
- The Mall at Partridge Creek, on Hall Road, is an open-air shopping center in Clinton Township with 3,250 parking spaces.
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mall_at_Partridge_Creek
- The township's address is 54111 Broughton Road (Township Hall). This is the point the drive time was measured from. The router puts it about half a mile along Broughton Road from 25 Mile Road.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/325/History-of-Macomb
- Car culture nearby, not in the township: the Clinton Township Gratiot Cruise runs along Gratiot Avenue between 14 Mile Road and Wellington Crescent. The 22nd edition was Sunday, August 3, 2025, with a car show, and a 23rd was being planned for 2026. It is in Clinton Township, the shop's home town.
  https://www.candgnews.com/news/cruising-the-avenue-8889
- Township Parks and Recreation events include Tons O' Trucks and a Santa Cruise. No car show or cruise run by the township was found.
  https://macomb-mi.gov/507/About-Parks-and-Recreation

## Drive

From docs/research/drive-times.json:

- measured_from: 54111 Broughton Rd, Macomb, MI (Township Hall)
- miles: 15.5
- minutes_no_traffic: 30
- On the pages: "about 15 miles from central Macomb Township" and "roughly 30 minutes without traffic".

Route: confirmed. I ran the same two points through the public OSRM router (OpenStreetMap data) and got 15.48 miles and 29.7 minutes, which matches the file, so the file's number is this route.
https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/driving/-82.9232204,42.6957208;-82.9260345,42.5403101?overview=false&steps=true&alternatives=true

- From Township Hall and the middle of the township: 25 Mile Road to Romeo Plank Road, Romeo Plank Road south (it crosses Hall Road and keeps going) to Cass Avenue, Cass Avenue to Groesbeck Highway (M-97), Groesbeck south to Kelly Road, Kelly Road to 14 Mile Road, east on 14 Mile Road a few hundred yards, Louise Ave on the left (north side).
- From the west side (checked from Garfield Road near 22 Mile): Garfield Road south all the way to 14 Mile Road, then east on 14 Mile Road past Groesbeck and Kelly to Louise Ave on the left. Two roads.
  https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/driving/-82.9562,42.6590;-82.9260345,42.5403101?overview=false&steps=true&alternatives=true
- From the east side (checked from near North Avenue and 22 Mile): North Avenue south, which becomes Groesbeck Highway (M-97) at Hall Road, then Groesbeck south to Kelly Road and 14 Mile Road.
  https://router.project-osrm.org/route/v1/driving/-82.8850,42.6600;-82.9260345,42.5403101?overview=false&steps=true
- Not checked by driving it. The router gives no-traffic times, and Romeo Plank is known to back up. Road work on Broughton, 24 Mile and (later) Romeo Plank can change the first few miles, so the pages name the main roads and do not promise a time beyond "roughly 30 minutes without traffic".

## Do not claim

Looked for and could not confirm. None of this goes on a page.

- Gravel or unpaved roads inside Macomb Township today. The county maintains about 335 miles of gravel road countywide, but none of the county's limestone program lists names a road in this township, and the only gravel reference found (24 Mile Road, 2015) was a segment being paved.
- That the county uses brine on paved roads in winter today. Salt is confirmed. Brine is confirmed for gravel road maintenance and in a 2007 article only.
- Tree cover, tree sap, mature trees or wooded lots. No source describes the township's tree canopy. The township was farmland.
- A lake, beach, boat launch or Metropark inside the township. None found. Stony Creek, Wolcott Mill and Lake St. Clair Metroparks are in other communities.
- Garages. The census confirms detached houses, not garages. The pages say "driveway", which is an inference from detached houses (flagged in doubts).
- Parking decks or street parking as a pattern. Nothing found either way.
- Large employers, plants or office parks inside the township. None confirmed. Do not say where residents work or how long they commute.
- A car show, cruise or car meet held in Macomb Township. Only the Gratiot Cruise in Clinton Township was confirmed.
- The east boundary road. Chesterfield Township is the neighbor, but no source names the line.
- Current status of the Romeo Plank widening, or any road closure by name. It changes month to month.
- Anything about HOA rules on service vehicles, or how many customers the shop has here.

Michigan Tint Law & FAQ

## Michigan Window Tint Law, Answered
Michigan's tint rules are different from almost every other state. Here's what to know before you book — and our specialists will walk you through the details for your exact vehicle during your consultation.

Is window tint legal in Michigan?
Yes — Michigan allows window tint, but the rules on where film can go and how dark it can be are specific to each set of windows, and they're different from most other states. During your initial consultation, our specialists will walk you through exactly what's legal for your vehicle and help you land on a setup that looks right and keeps you compliant — including your options for darker rear windows and Michigan's medical exemption process if you have a light-sensitive condition. The final shade choice is always yours.

How long does window tint installation take?
Most installs are done the same day you drop off:
- Front 2 windows: about 1.5 hours
- Full sides: about 2.5 hours
- Full sides + rear glass: about 3.5 hours
- Front windshield: about 1.5 hours

When can I roll my windows down after tinting?
Give it 3–4 days before rolling tinted windows down so the film fully bonds to the glass. You may notice small water pockets or a light haze during that first week — that's completely normal and clears on its own as the film cures. Rolling the windows down too early is the one thing that can peel a fresh edge, so set a reminder and leave them up.

Will my tint bubble or turn purple over time?
Cheap dyed film does — that purple, bubbling look is the dye breaking down. We don't install it. Our Geoshield C2 Carbon and Ceramic films are color-stable and backed by a lifetime warranty covering bubbling, peeling, and fading. Combined with plotter-cut patterns and an in-shop install, the tint on your glass should outlast your ownership of the vehicle.

Can you remove my old tint?
Yes — old, purple, or bubbling tint is stripped using controlled heat and adhesive removal that protects your rear defroster lines , then the glass is cleaned to a factory-fresh finish, ready for new film. Removal starts at $50 per area , and it's 50% off when we install a full-vehicle tint the same day .

## Your Car Isn't Dirty the Way a City Car Is Dirty

Shelby Township sits where the subdivisions stop and the gravel starts. That
mix shows up on your paint and in your carpet in a very specific way — and it's
why a $20 tunnel wash on Hall Road never quite gets it.

- Limestone dust from the north-end gravel roads. Above 25 Mile, that fine white film settles into door jambs, wheel faces, and the lower third of the paint. It's abrasive — wiping it off dry is how swirl marks start.

- Winter brine off M-53 and Hall Road. Liquid de-icer creeps into rocker panels and dries as a white crust on floor mats. Salt keeps eating long after the snow's gone unless it's extracted, not vacuumed.

- Stony Creek in the cargo area. Trail dust, wet dog, muddy cleats, kayak drip, and bike grease. Metropark weekends are hard on a third row.

- Tree cover and sap. Older Shelby subdivisions have mature canopy — sap, pollen wash, and bird bombing bond to clear coat fast in a July heat wave.

- Long commutes south. Van Dyke to the Tech Center or M-59 to Auburn Hills means highway film, bug splatter, and iron fallout from brake dust that a wash mitt just slides over.

## Detailing in Shelby Township, Answered

The questions we get most from customers on this side of Macomb County.

Do you actually come out to Shelby Township, or do I have to drive to Clinton Township?

We come to you. Shelby Township is inside our core mobile radius — we cover 48315, 48316, and 48317, from the Utica end up past 26 Mile. You can also drop the vehicle at our shop at 33115 Louise Ave in Clinton Township, or use our pick-up and drop-off valet service and never leave home. Machine polishing, ceramic coating, and window tint are shop services for quality reasons, but valet covers that too.

How much does car detailing cost in Shelby Township?

Interior details start at $184.99, exterior details at $139.99, and full interior-plus-exterior packages at $249.99, all for a coupe or sedan in average condition. Shelby Township skews toward trucks and three-row SUVs, which are quoted at a higher tier because there is more surface to clean. There is no separate travel charge for Shelby Township addresses. Financing is available through Affirm and Klarna.

What do you need from me for a mobile appointment at my house?

A driveway or parking spot with enough room to walk the full perimeter of the vehicle. We bring our own power and water, so you don't need to run a cord or a hose. Please pull the vehicle out of the garage if you can — natural light helps our technician see what they're correcting. If your subdivision has HOA rules about service vehicles, let us know when you book and we'll work around them.

Can you get the gravel dust and road salt out?

Yes, and they need different treatments. Gravel and limestone dust on the exterior comes off with a proper decontamination wash — iron removal and clay bar — rather than a wipe-down, because dragging that grit across clear coat is exactly how swirl marks appear. Road salt inside the vehicle needs hot-water extraction to pull it out of the carpet fibers, which is included in our Executive Interior and all full-vehicle packages. A vacuum alone leaves the salt behind and it keeps working on the carpet backing.

My car takes a beating from Stony Creek weekends. Can you handle dog hair, mud, and wet gear?

That's a large share of what we do out here. Steam and a two-step shampoo process lift embedded pet hair, dried mud, and the sources of most odors out of seats and carpet instead of covering them with fragrance. Some things are permanent — dye transfer, old bleach marks — and we tell you honestly during the walkaround what will and won't come out. Heavy pet hair or biohazard cleanup can add time, which we confirm with you before starting.

When is the best time of year to book a detail in Michigan?

Two windows matter most: late March through April, to get the winter brine out before it does long-term damage, and September, to clear a summer of sap, bugs, and UV exposure before the salt trucks come back out. Those are our busiest weeks, so book a couple weeks ahead. If you have kids, dogs, or a long commute down Van Dyke, a maintenance visit every one to three months keeps the vehicle from ever needing a full restoration again — ask about our maintenance plan pricing.

How long does it take, and do I lose the car for the day?

An exterior Wash & Seal runs about an hour and a half, a Premium Interior about two hours, and a full Signature Refresh about five. Larger vehicles and anything with machine polishing takes longer. With a mobile appointment you keep working while we're in the driveway; with valet, we handle both trips. We give you a firm time estimate when you book.

Do I need a detail before a ceramic coating?

No — every ceramic coating we install already includes the decontamination and paint prep the coating needs, so you don't book a separate exterior detail first. If you want the interior handled during the same appointment, the Signature or Luxury Refresh pairs well with a coating. See the ceramic coating options →

Should I clean out my car before you show up?

Just take out personal items, valuables, and car seats from the cabin and trunk. You don't need to clean anything — that's the job — but clearing loose belongings lets our technician reach every surface and keeps your things safe. Anything you forget, we set aside for you.

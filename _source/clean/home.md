## Questions, Answered
The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

What services do you offer? +
We offer ceramic coating, window tinting, auto detailing, and paint correction, plus mobile and valet service if you can't make it to the shop.

What areas do you serve? +
We're based in Clinton Township and serve all of Macomb and Oakland counties, including Sterling Heights, Troy, Rochester Hills, Shelby Township, Birmingham, and Royal Oak.

Do I have to bring my car in, or can you come to me? +
Both work. You can drop off at our shop, or take advantage of our mobile and valet service — we'll pick up your vehicle, service it, and bring it back to you.

How much do your services cost? +
Pricing depends on the service and your vehicle. Ceramic coating packages start at $449 for our 1-year application; detailing, tint, and correction pricing varies by vehicle size and condition. We'll confirm exact pricing when you request your quote.

Do you offer financing? +
Yes — we offer financing through Affirm and Klarna, so you can pay over 3 to 24 months, as low as 0% interest.

How long does an appointment take? +
It depends on the service. A detail can often be done same-day, while ceramic coating typically takes a full day in the shop between correction, application, and cure time. We'll give you a specific timeline when you book.

Is there a warranty? +
Yes, our ceramic coating packages are backed by a written warranty. Ask about the specifics for your vehicle and tier when you request your quote.

Do you have any current specials? +
Yes — book a paint coating package before October 1st and we'll ceramic coat your windshield, door glass, rear glass, and rims for free.

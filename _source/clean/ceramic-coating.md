## What a ceramic coating does for your paint — and what it doesn't

Whether this is your first coating or your third, here's the straight version.

### Think of it as a hard, clear shell for your paint

A ceramic coating is a liquid that's applied by hand to your paint, then
hardens into a thin, glass-like layer that bonds to the clear coat .
Once it cures, that layer is what takes the abuse instead of your paint.

Wax sits on top of the paint and washes off in weeks. A coating becomes part
of the surface and lasts for years . Here's what you'll actually notice:

- Water and dirt slide off. Rain beads up and runs, so the car stays cleaner between washes.

- Washing takes half the time. Grime doesn't grip the surface, so a rinse and a gentle wash does the job.

- Michigan winters are less of a threat. Road salt, brine, and slush don't get to sit against bare clear coat.

- Bird droppings, bugs, and tree sap wipe off before they etch the paint.

- Deeper gloss. Your paint looks wet and rich, and stays that way.

What it won't do: a coating isn't armor. It won't stop rock chips or a key scratch, and it isn't a substitute for washing. It makes your paint far easier to keep looking new — that's the job.

I know coatings

### The coating is only as good as the prep underneath it

You already know a coating locks in whatever's under it. That's why we treat
prep as the job and application as the last step. Every paint package at
Refresh My Ride is applied by certified technicians in a
controlled shop environment, and here's what happens before the bottle opens:

- Chemical + mechanical decontamination. Iron and tar removal, then clay to pull embedded contaminants out of the clear coat.

- Paint correction included. Swirls, light scratches, and oxidation get machine-polished out before coating — not sealed in.

- Panel wipe. Polishing oils stripped so the coating bonds directly to bare clear coat.

- Controlled application and cure. Applied panel by panel, flashed and leveled, then cured in-shop before your vehicle leaves.

- Written warranty, aftercare guide, and a follow-up wash so the first maintenance wash is done right and the coating is inspected.

Package tiers: The Refresh (1-year), The Revival (3-year), and The Renewal (5-year), chosen by how long you plan to keep the vehicle and how it's stored and driven. We'll walk you through which one fits on the quote call — not upsell you into the longest one.

The coating bonds to the clear coat and becomes the surface everything touches. Water beads, contaminants can't grip, and the paint underneath stays sealed.

Common Questions

## Ceramic coating questions, answered straight

The things people ask us most before booking. If yours isn't here, call and ask.

How long does a ceramic coating actually last?

We offer 1, 3, and 5-year packages, and those numbers are what the coating is built to hold up to with normal use and proper washing. The bigger factors are how the vehicle is stored and how it's washed — a garaged car that gets hand-washed will outlast one that lives outside and goes through a brush tunnel wash.
Every package comes with a written warranty and an aftercare guide so you know exactly how to keep it performing.

How much does a ceramic coating cost?

It depends on your specific vehicle — size, the current condition of the paint, and how much correction it needs before coating. A well-kept sedan and a work truck with heavy swirls are very different jobs, so a price list would just be a guess.

How long will my car be at your shop?

Most coating jobs are a multi-day stay. The coating itself goes on relatively quickly — it's the decontamination, paint correction, and cure time that take the hours, and rushing any of them shows in the finish.
We'll give you an exact window when we quote your vehicle. Uber is covered for drop-off and pickup, and we offer pick-up and drop-off valet service if you'd rather not come to us at all.

Is paint correction included, or is that extra?

Correction is included with every coating package. It's not an upsell — it's the step that makes the coating worth doing.
A coating is clear and it bonds to whatever's underneath. Apply it over swirls and scratches and you've sealed those in for years. We polish the defects out first, then coat. Heavily damaged paint may need a more involved multi-stage correction, which we'll flag during the quote rather than surprise you with later.

Do I still have to wash my car?

Yes — a coating makes washing easier, not optional. Dirt and salt don't bond to the surface the way they do to bare clear coat, so a rinse and a gentle hand wash gets the car clean in about half the time.
What we do ask: skip the brush-style automatic washes. Those spinning brushes put swirls into any finish, coated or not. Touchless is fine.

Will a coating stop rock chips and scratches?

No, and anyone telling you otherwise is overselling. A ceramic coating is a hard, thin chemical barrier — it resists chemical etching, UV fade, salt, and light marring from washing. It is not physical impact protection.
If rock chips on the front end are your main concern, that's what paint protection film is for. Ask us and we'll tell you honestly which one fits what you're worried about.

Can you coat a brand new car? What about an older one?

Both, and new is the ideal time — the paint is in its best condition, so there's less correction needed and you're protecting it from day one. New cars still get decontaminated and prepped; factory paint is rarely as clean as it looks.
Older vehicles absolutely can be coated. If the paint is faded or oxidized, correction is doing more work up front, but the result on a tired finish is often the more dramatic of the two.

How soon after coating can I wash it or drive in the rain?

The coating cures in our shop before you pick the vehicle up, so normal driving is fine right away, rain included. There's a short window afterward where we ask you to hold off on washing while the coating fully hardens.
The exact timing is in the aftercare guide you get at pickup, and your free follow-up wash is scheduled so the first real wash is done correctly by us.

Can you coat more than just the paint?

Yes. Windshield and exterior glass, wheels, and trim can all be coated, and doing them in the same visit is far more efficient than coming back later.
Coated glass is the one most people underrate — rain sheets off at highway speed and your wipers do noticeably less work through a Michigan winter.

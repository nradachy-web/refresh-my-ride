## A Real Detail, Not a Car Wash With Extra Steps
Michigan is hard on a car — road salt in the floor mats, pollen in the vents, iron fallout bonded to the paint. A drive-through wash moves dirt around; a Refresh My Ride detail removes it. Our trained, quality-checked technicians work a step-by-step process for every package: interiors get extracted, steamed, and deodorized, not just vacuumed, and exteriors get a full decontamination (iron removal, clay bar, bug, tar, and sap) before any protection goes on. Book it however it fits your week — at our Clinton Township shop, in your driveway with mobile service, or hand us the keys with pick-up & drop-off valet.
- Interior deep clean — hot-water extraction, steam, and odor removal for seats, carpet, and trim
- Exterior decontamination — iron, clay bar, bug, tar, and sap removed, not buried under wax
- Paint polishing and scratch removal — scratches, swirls, oxidation, and more, removed using a machine polisher

## Car Detailing Questions, Answered
Straight answers about what a detail includes, what it costs, how long it takes, and how to keep your car looking like it just left the shop.
Still have a question? Call (586) 400-1303

What's the difference between a car detail and a car wash?

A car wash removes loose dirt from the outside of the car in a few minutes. A detail is a multi-hour, hands-on cleaning of every surface. On the exterior, that means a full decontamination (iron removal, clay bar, bug, tar, and sap) before any sealant goes on. On the interior, it means vacuuming every crevice, cleaning and conditioning all surfaces, and on our deeper packages, steam cleaning and extracting seats and carpet. A wash makes a car look clean from ten feet; a detail makes it clean up close.

How much does car detailing cost in Clinton Township?

Our interior details start at $184.99, exterior details start at $139.99, and full interior-plus-exterior packages start at $249.99, all priced for a coupe or sedan in average condition. SUVs and trucks are quoted for their size. If your vehicle needs more than the package covers, such as heavy pet hair or set-in stains, we explain any adjustment and get your approval before work begins. Financing is available through Affirm and Klarna.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the package and the vehicle. An exterior Wash & Seal takes around an hour and a half, a Premium Interior Detail around two hours, and a full Signature Refresh around five hours. Larger vehicles and packages with machine polishing take longer. We give you a time estimate when you book, and if you use our pick-up and drop-off valet service, you don't have to wait at all.

Do you offer mobile detailing, or do I have to come to the shop?

Both, plus a third option. You can drop your vehicle at our Clinton Township shop, we can come to your home or workplace anywhere in Macomb or Oakland County with mobile service, or we can pick your vehicle up, detail it at the shop, and bring it back with our valet service. Mobile appointments need a driveway or parking spot with room to work around the vehicle. We confirm the details when you book.

Can you remove pet hair, stains, and odors?

Yes. Our Executive Interior Detail and full-vehicle packages include steam cleaning and a 2-step shampoo process, which lifts embedded pet hair, food and drink stains, and the sources of most odors out of seats and carpet rather than covering them with fragrance. Some stains are permanent, such as dye transfer or old bleach marks, and we tell you honestly what will and won't come out during the walkaround. Heavy pet hair or biohazard cleanup may add time, which we always confirm with you first.

How often should I get my car detailed?

For most drivers in Michigan, a full detail once or twice a year, timed around the end of winter and the end of summer, keeps road salt, pollen, and UV damage from building up. If you have kids, pets, or a long commute, a maintenance detail every one to three months keeps the car from ever getting bad enough to need a full restoration again. Ask us about our maintenance plans, which lock in a lower per-visit price for regular service.

Do I need to clean out my car before the appointment?

Please remove personal items, valuables, car seats, and anything you want to keep from the cabin and trunk before we start. You don't need to clean anything, that's our job, but clearing out loose belongings lets our technicians get to every surface and protects your things. If you forget something, we set it aside for you.

Do you detail trucks, SUVs, and luxury vehicles?

Yes. We work on everything from daily-driver sedans to full-size trucks, three-row SUVs, and luxury and exotic vehicles. Larger vehicles are quoted at a higher tier because there is simply more surface to clean, and our technicians are trained on the leather, wood, and delicate trim found in higher-end interiors.

Should I get a detail before a ceramic coating?

Every ceramic coating we install already includes the decontamination and paint prep the coating needs, so you don't need to book a separate detail first. If you want the interior handled at the same time, our Luxury Refresh or Signature Refresh packages pair well with a coating appointment. Read about our ceramic coating options
to see which protection level fits your car.
